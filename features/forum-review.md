# Forum Review

**A queue with a history, not just a pile of forum posts.**

GlobalHost uses a review workflow for selected forums so staff can track submissions, make decisions and see where the process is getting stuck. It is part of the private bot built for this server.

## From submission to decision

- New submissions in configured forums enter the review workflow.
- Reviews can be pending, approved, denied or blacklisted.
- Staff can inspect past reviews and recorded decisions.
- Pending reviews can receive reminders when they have waited too long.
- Changes to approved submissions can be recorded as revisions.

## Tools for reviewers

- **/forumstats** summarises submissions, decisions, decision rates and average review time over a selected period.
- **/forumhistory** searches past reviews and can filter by status or member.
- **/forumhealth** reports processing counts, failures, retries and timing information.
- **/solved** lets a forum question be marked as solved in its thread.

## AI assists. Staff decide.

Automated analysis can provide reviewers with extra context, but it does **not** make the final approval, denial or blacklist decision. Human reviewers remain responsible for the outcome.

## What makes this different?

Discord forums organise posts, but do not provide this complete review workflow by themselves: decision history, reminders, review statistics and processing-health information. GlobalHost connects those tools with the wider moderation and community toolkit.
