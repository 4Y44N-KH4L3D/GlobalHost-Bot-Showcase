# Support Tickets

**A support request should not disappear into chat history.**

The GlobalHost server uses a ticket system to give members a clear way to contact staff and help the support team manage requests from opening through closure. It is part of our private server bot.

## The workflow

- **Open:** members use an interactive ticket panel to choose a request type and start a private support conversation.
- **Assign:** staff can claim or release a ticket, transfer it, or invite another member when extra help is needed.
- **Prioritise:** staff can change a ticket's priority and add internal notes that stay out of the member conversation.
- **Resolve:** staff can close or reopen tickets, inspect ticket details and search ticket records.
- **Review:** support statistics help staff understand ticket activity. The Owner can export analytics for further analysis.

Ticket deletion retains its audit record, and supported ticket records persist in the bot's database rather than relying only on visible channel history.

## Why the workflow matters

A private channel is only the start. Assignment, transfers, priority, internal notes and searchable records help staff hand requests over clearly and follow up on unresolved issues.

## Permissions and privacy

Ticket access depends on configured staff roles and Discord channel permissions. The bot needs permission to create and manage ticket channels. Staff should handle sensitive information carefully and avoid putting secrets into internal notes or transcripts.

## Compared with a basic ticket bot

A basic ticket bot may be enough for private channels and a close button. GlobalHost adds handovers, internal notes, priority, records and analytics to the ticket workflow.
