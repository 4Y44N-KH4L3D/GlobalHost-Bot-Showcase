# Why GlobalHost?

**A place to learn, make things and meet other developers.**

GlobalHost is a developer-focused Discord community. It is for people who want to talk about programming, ask questions, share projects and learn from other people who are interested in technology.

The point is the community itself, not a bot. The custom bot is something we built to help run our own server, and it is not available for other servers to invite.

## What you can get involved in

### Talk about development

Discuss programming languages, tools, software design, backend development, Linux, infrastructure, cybersecurity and other areas of tech. Share what you are learning and compare different ways to solve a problem.

### Ask for help and help others

Use the community to talk through errors, get another perspective on a problem or help someone else figure something out. Clear questions and useful replies make the server better for everyone.

### Share projects and get feedback

Post things you are building, discuss design choices and get feedback from other members. Projects do not have to be finished to be worth sharing.

### Find people with similar interests

Whether you're mainly interested in Python, web development, backend systems, Linux, AI, game development or another area, GlobalHost gives you a place to meet people who share those interests.

### Take part in a managed community

Moderation, support channels, forum review and events help keep the server organised. The aim is to make it easier to find discussions, ask questions and take part without the server becoming a mess.

## What makes GlobalHost different?

- **Built around developers.** The community is centred on programming, projects and technology rather than being a general-purpose chat server.
- **Room to learn at different levels.** You can ask beginner questions, share what you know or discuss more advanced technical topics.
- **Projects are part of the conversation.** The server is a place to work through ideas and share progress, not just drop links.
- **Tools support the community.** The custom bot helps with moderation, tickets, forum review and other server tasks, but the people and conversations are the main point.

## About the custom bot

GlobalHost uses a private bot built for this server. It includes AutoMod, raid alerts and staff-controlled lockdown, moderation commands, support tickets, forum review, events and developer search tools.

The bot's documentation is public so people can see how the server's tools work. **It is not a public bot, and you cannot add it to another server.** The showcase repository contains documentation, not the production source code.

## Is GlobalHost for you?

If you're interested in programming, building projects, learning about tech or helping other developers, GlobalHost may be a good fit. The best way to see whether it suits you is to join the community and take part.
